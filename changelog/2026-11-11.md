# 2026-11-11 — Roadmap improvements

## Gravity readings

- Added deletion of individual gravity readings from batch history, brewing
  insights, and vessel pages. Graph users can reveal the delete control by
  hovering over or selecting a reading; list users can delete from their
  existing readings list. A confirmation dialog prevents accidental deletion.
- Graph controls support touch and keyboard access, and dismiss when the
  pointer leaves or the user clicks outside, as appropriate.
- Added authenticated, batch-scoped deletion to `app/api/sg/route.ts` and
  shared deletion and list components. No database migration is required.

## Brew-day checklists

- Split beer checklists by brewing method. Fresh wort and malt extract kits
  have their own preparation instructions, without boil or chilling steps or
  timers; all-grain retains its existing timed steps.
- Filter obsolete completed steps and timers from saved checklist state when
  loading a checklist. Added regression tests and English and Norwegian text.

## Profile overview and brewing mix

- Centered the brewing overview layout when the Ratings statistic is hidden.
- Kept batch and recipe brewing-mix charts side by side on mobile when both
  exist; a single chart remains centered.
- Made chart segments selectable by mouse, touch, and keyboard. Selection
  emphasizes the segment and its label, and clears on a second selection or
  outside click. Keyboard focus uses a curved highlight rather than a square
  outline.

## Admin roadmap

- Added a confirmed bulk action to clear completed roadmap entries, with
  administrator authorization and an audit record. Individual deletion remains
  available.
- Removed success messages for adding and completing roadmap entries; errors
  remain visible.
